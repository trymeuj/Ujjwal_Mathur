Not the Right Founder—Yet

I am a founder and people say that I am a young founder and I sometimes tend to agree with them (I am 21 while writing this).
I graduated from college this month and this is my 2nd startup (first Svar and now Aiva). I've pitched to dozens of investors, tried raising money for both, and whatever small wins I've had, I still wouldn't call it "raising a round."

I got $30K for Svar, but from competitions and grants, not investors. Sometimes, it's "you are too early", sometimes "small TAM", and several other stuff that investors say. And I realise these are things investors say to get around and dodge. It's never too early, it's never a small TAM, it's never the market or stage or product really. People raise pre-product, they raise in niche businesses. It's always, the Founder. It's always "You are not right for us, yet".

And I have been used to listening, "Sorry, We have decided to pass", and now it goes into my ears like - "You are not the right founder, yet". The Yet.
That yet used to sting. Now I'm starting to appreciate it. Because they're right—I haven't earned it yet. I'm not the founder who makes it a no-brainer. Not the one who walks into the room and flips the question from "Why should we invest?" to "How can we not?"
Not yet.
Right now, not even I can say that for myself :). And I am ready to take accountability for that, I won't be blind.

Everytime, I realise, I am not yet the guy who can sell a concept. And this is what I am really loving about building a startup. Love the mirror it always keeps in front of you if you decide to untie the blindfold from your eyes.

And I love the opportunity it presents. The rejections I face always end with a "Yet". I am not a great founder, yet. I am not the best seller, yet. I am not the right guy to invest in, yet. I am becoming that founder, past the yet. There will always be more to learn, I am getting past "more yets" everyday. Love it.

I also worry, what would have happened if I hadn't started up out of courage or naivety, whichever is the case with me 😀. I wouldn't have known how many yets there are in my life.

Love it.

Thanks for reading.
