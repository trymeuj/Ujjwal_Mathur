Don't Sweat the Small Stuff

Credits: Richard Carlson

This essay is directly a praise of "Don't Sweat the Small Stuff... and It's all small stuff" by Dr Richard Carlson. I read it recently and it's been one of the simplest and best reads I've had.

Reading about the "small stuff" made me question myself and observe my own behaviour towards it. How do I handle the "small stuff" ? Just every other person. Making a big deal about the small stuff. I noticed that I live life like a big emergency, and consider every event or task like a must complete or must win task.

Now, a perspective is that winning these small events eventually fetches big results, and that's very true. In no way, I am denying that. However, what I am pointing out is how we make a big deal about the results of the events, after they are done or the time has passed by. Even during the event, we extract the joy out of it, worrying, and meanwhile deprive ourselves from participating in other things.

A cliche example, preparing for an exam. This happens way too often with me. If I have an exam the day after tomorrow, and my friend calls me for an hour of gym or FIFA session, I always deny, only to later waste 2 hours on "miscellaneous" activity that I don't have any account for. It's just that I won't have the "guilt" that I went for FIFA and that I wasted my time. Haha, funny nature of mine. Please smile if you can relate to this funny nature. Well, personally, this is not just about exams, but a lot many parts of my day, and only on self-introspection I observed this in myself.

This abstract concept of sweating about small stuff or wins can be easily expanded to a range of behavioural traits. Think of a time when someone was telling a story or sharing an achievement, how we often immediately tried to share something about ourselves. Just pause and think.

Smile again if you can relate. I can totally relate. This is just human nature, everyone wants attention and wants to feel important or special. We can try to be easy sometimes, and let it go.

Let Others Have the Glory.

Richard Carlson has talked about a lot of relatable small stuff that we sweat about in his "Don't Sweat the Small Stuff... and it's all small stuff". A good and small read that triggers self-introspection.
