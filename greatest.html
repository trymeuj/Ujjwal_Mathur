Who are the greatest sportsperson of all time ?

I am very confused right now, I just saw a clip on LinkedIn, an interview of Novak Djokovic. I always chose Rafa over Novak, so personally, I didn’t like him. And especially when he surpassed Rafa for the “most grand slams for an individual” record. I kinda disliked him, and then I sympathized with myself, because this had happened with me before. Technically, not with me, but people I admired and some whom even I idealised. Cristiano was surpassed by Messi for the most Ballon D’ors. Lebron, very arguably, surpassed Jordan, a lot of people do not agree with this and even I don’t, Michael is still THE basketball icon for me. But the fact that he was The basketball icon for everyone. Floyd surpassed Ali and Tyson, and similarly many others. I had seen this, my hero becoming number 2, and that led to a lot of people disrespecting them, specially on social media. I didn’t like this, and I sympathised with myself for being the victim here.

Initially, I disliked and sometimes even hated these greats, especially Messi, because his claim was the strongest among all the other surpassers, especially with the World Cup. But I came to realize and acknowledge the greatness, not just of my heroes and those who surpassed them, but also of others who are deemed greats, those who have done what others could not have done (subjectively). “You have got to acknowledge them”, I said to myself once in thought. You cannot forget Maradona. You cannot forget Agassi. You cannot not acknowledge Kobe. You simply cannot. These people are the greatest people in the areas they set their foot in, accept it or not.

There cannot be many “greatest”, oh I am not making a grammatical error, I will address that.

But the thing I stuck with me, was the moment I realised that I have to accept them. How do you prove Messi is greater than Ronaldo? It’s very very subjective, the golden balls do not define who’s better, people vote for it, it depends on people, and the moment something depends on people, it becomes who’s more likable, and not better. I am not defending Cristiano here, he might or might not be the greatest. Messi might be a greater footballer, and even Maradona might be the greatest. Who decides that? The super ballon d’or was given to Stefano and he played in the same era as Pele and Maradona, and he was nowhere in the Player of the century debate. Maradona won the player of the 20th century award when over 50 percent of people voted for him. Then a football committee voted Pele as the best, with over 70 percent votes. But wait Pele won 3 world cups, the most by anyone ever, ever. Then why isn’t he the best till date? Probably because nobody who is alive today has seen him play, well that’s the internet nature of people’s perception.

The crux is, at least what I believe in, we/one cannot decide who’s the greatest in such fields, given there is so much subjectivity, the voters, the parameters, the weights of parameters. Ah, we/one cannot. I don’t know why this superlative was defined, or it was termed by the self proclaimed wannabes, I don't know. But the way I think about it, is we can define a pool of greatests, and that pool, a very small one can be called The Greatest (as a metaphor). Don’t take it wrong, this isn’t like the hall of fame with a hundred people in it. It’s too large to be even called a group, so, pool.

Cristiano, Leo, Pele, Messi get in. Sorry Zizou, sorry Eusebio and Baggio. Jordan, Lebron, Kobe get in there. Probably even Bill Russel. I am not randomly naming athletes, instead I thought of who can be in this pool. People who are strongly in the goat race might be the ones. A subjective parameter again. But you see, I am not trying to be specific to names or individuals. Just like when I wanted Cristiano to win the debate, I blindly wanted that. But now, I might even accept a pool with no Cristiano in it, some day. Because I simply cannot admire an individual more than the idea of The Greatest pool. The pool stands out among the plethora of humans competing. And the core idea or trait, common among all the pools, think beyond sports, is greatness.

Abruptly, but getting back to the clip I saw. It was Novak speaking about his or greats’ mentality. He said there is no room for doubts, no second thoughts, you have to believe in yourself, like insanely, and that he said is the difference, he thinks, between people who are and who are not at the highest level. Brutal, but probably the truth. That put me into an internal discussion, there must be so many people who have had this mentality and who are not among the greats’, let alone the pool. I think these people in the pool might not be the best people to listen to when talking about the non-negotiables about reaching that level, just as IITians are not the best people to listen to for clearing IIT, a more relatable example. Not saying they are wrong, but just trying to establish explicitly that they do not have the mantras. Because whenever you listen to the interviews of these people, Kobe, Novak, Floyd, it gets you fired up, gets me too, and it feels like you have figured out the key, but I don’t believe that. I think it’s just the adrenaline that gets us overwhelmed, and out of reality….. To be continued
