Why do we settle for less?

First things first. One wants love and appreciation from people, right?

People want to win, right? Everyone wants to win. And what does success demand? Efforts. On and off the field.

But we can often see people settling for sympathy. What does sympathy demand? Efforts. On the field. Although I have had just a handful of experiences around here. I mean I am 21, far away from the extremely experienced people you would read sharing their perspectives. But this scenario is so common that even a guy who hasn’t even casted his first vote can say this. I do see people settling. Settling for less. If not success, then sympathy. Something’s better than nothing after all. Just go out and you can observe multiple such events within a day, just find some challenges to watch out for. Where there are challenges, there are people settling.

The reason? I can think of two. One is about what it takes and the other is how does it feel. The feel is good, definitely. I have had that feel quite a few times myself. What do I have to do? I just have to show that I am trying. And there will be people around ready with their “it’s okay”s. The “it’s okay” feels good, especially if it comes from several people.

The summary, I tried, I failed, people appreciated me.

And I guess that’s what we are thriving for. For appreciation and love from people. Sympathy gets confused with love. I start believing that these people like me. Actually they do not, at least not for this stint of mine.

The other reason. I think that’s the bigger reason. Because it takes a lot. Among the two components of efforts: On and off the field. On-field efforts bring glory, off-field efforts bring agony. On-field efforts win us appreciation, off-field efforts have big opportunity costs attached to them.

So, we got 2 deals in front of us…and for now, we take out the risk, just to keep the equation simple.

Deal 1: Go all in. Work hard, on the field, and especially, especially off the field. Do 100% of the work, and get 100% rewards.

Deal 2: Work partially. Make on field efforts, which is something like just 5% of the total, and get rewards aka sympathy, which feels like at least 20% of the rewards, at least in the moment, and well, we are short sighted beings, so we are okay with our moment.

Expected values ?

Does it make sense now? I guess it does.

So, why not just make the on field efforts, which is just 5% of the total time, and settle for sympathy, which creates an illusion of partial success. Hard truth, it is not. People appreciate failures. I do it personally. Failures teach you a lot, and more importantly, it takes courage to attempt and fail.

It might sound like I am criticizing both the faces of the coin. But if you think about it, I am criticizing the “willingness to settle”. Settling actually means, you didn’t have the courage, you didn’t attempt, and you did not fail. Embrace failures, for sure do. But do not call settling as failing. Failing is having the courage to try, try, keep trying. You do not accept failure until you fail. …. To be continued
