Hard Skills or Soft Skills

Credits: Sam Altman, Ben Horowitz

I was reading Sam Altman's blog, "How to be successful", and never once did he mention a hard skill (or the importance of any hard skill to master) to focus on. There were points like compounding, self-belief, drive, etc. and the one that best matches the criterion of hard skill was, "be good as sales".

Anyone would follow Sam's advice to be successful, so let's treat his advice as if it's set in stone.

So, roughly, the skills to focus are soft skills, yet nowhere around me at least I see people working on or even discussing these. Nobody mentions that we should be really internally driven, or should focus on independent thinking. Mostly we are talking about what skill to get good at, like programming or design.

But let's leave this topic aside of why we don't discuss or not focus on that. Let's say I've figured out that I need to really focus on being driven, being self-confident and learning independent thinking. And this is really good advice, and great to hear in principle, but how do I really do it in practice?

I actually got to thinking about this and then decided to write about it.

How do we learn independent thinking, really ? What's step 1 or step x, anything ?

Maybe building things is one way. I have a very rough idea and that's because I've thought about it up until this point and I believe thinking more will get me more ideas. The best thing about reading such stuff from great people is that it reminds me of this "actually" important stuff.

On the contrary, I remember what Ben Horowitz says in "The hard thing about hard things", that there is no playbook for doing hard things, and maybe learning independent thinking is a "hard thing". Maybe, I am just digging dry ground trying to find this way of learning these things. But still, a starting point would be good at least.

I'll think more. I really wanted if I could discuss such things with friends here in college. Anyways, I hope work life is better at this.

Until then, thanks Sam. Will thank you in person soon. Haha, self-belief I guess :).

Thank You for Reading.
