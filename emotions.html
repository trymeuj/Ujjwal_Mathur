There are some emotions I don't understand

There are a lot of things I don’t understand. There are several emotions that I don’t understand. There are some scenarios that I don’t understand.

I am feeling one of those right now.

I am watching the post-match presentation of the T20 World Cup Final, 2024. India vs South Africa. Close to a billion, literally a billion, people wanted one common thing. I see Irfan Pathan crying in the studio, yes they are airing it. He is nostalgic about the time he won it for India. He is overwhelmed. The celebrations show Rohit Sharma crying, the fans crying, Rahul Dravid smiling, Jasprit Bumrah smiling. Everyone is experiencing a plethora of emotions, everyone is overwhelmed. Even the people who were watching it on their devices. A lot of people shared smiles, a lot of people drained tears. And why won’t they? They, everyone who is a citizen, awaited this moment. They wanted their team to lift the silverware. But the wait sort of became eternal when India lost the One Day World Cup on November 19, 2023. We saw the tears back then, for the first time (actually, in the eyes of a lot of people), probably after 2017. The dusk of the Legends Virat and Rohit triggered those emotions, as this was surely the last World Cup the two played together, having won nothing in their legends era. So much so that the word Depression became famous on social media in India probably because of those events.

So, these emotions have a rich history. But there are some outliers I saw.

Virat is not smiling, at least not a smile I feel to be true. It was a made-up smile when he received the Player Of the Match. An explanation might be the announcement of this being his last T20I game.

He has had an outstanding career. I am not someone to speak about that. He is the one guy I so wanted to win this trophy. A big reason I think is the RCB saga, because he hasn’t won any trophy since 2013. Rohit has won 5 since then. So, that’s an explanation of Virat being the favorite, supported by me being a Virat fan.

Anyway, as soon as I saw that presentation of Virat while receiving that award, my mind rewind to that interview of Virat Kohli where he addressed why he was laughing when he hit his awaited 71st century after 1019 days. He said he laughed, because he couldn’t believe this is what he had been waiting for. This is what he was starving for almost 3 years. Everyone criticised him, to the extent that he should be dropped from the team. Can you believe this, drop Virat Kohli from the Indian Team. Yes, this was a serious dining table debate for a long time. People who have followed Cricket for long were, at some point, part of this debate, and many even supported, and in fact justified, the proposal.

But really, I don’t understand this. I followed the One Day World Cup 2023 very closely. I had my semester exams before and after the finals, but you know just how important they are, If you have India playing against Australia in a WC final. In fact, I finished my exam early, to watch as much Cricket as possible that day. Like a lot of cricket fans, I wanted India to win so bad that day. But we lost. And that created a void, not just in me, but inside every cricket lover.

And that void, that wound reappeared today, in the hope that it might get replenished. And the result was expected to do that. But now that we have won the silverware, I am not overwhelmed anymore. I don’t understand, I don’t feel what I was feeling every moment I thought India was losing, when it was 30 off 30, not when Surya got out. I went on a roller coaster throughout the match, but I don’t feel a high now. Virat bhai isn’t probably feeling that high. He was laughing out of the situation when he hit that century.

That triggered a question in me. Perhaps, we often don’t want the high, we just want the void to fill. We don’t feel the high or the plateau. There is no plateau in fact. There is either a void or a no void. That very well resembles the psychological concept of loss aversion. We see and feel a loss much more than a gain or win of equivalent volume.

I have felt this before. You might have too maybe. There might have been a time when you wanted something so hard that you made so much effort to get there, but when you get there, you feel like, now what. I am here, now what.

I don’t have an answer or even a side to take on this. This is just a speculation. But I do want answers. I will surely write more on this when I have enough.

I don’t want to keep the feeling of having that void away from this. I saw Quinton de Kock not crying in 2015. I saw him crying in 2023. I saw him crying today. I saw Captain Markram. The proteas tasted the fluid filling up the void, only to find it digged even deeper. Reminded me of De Villiers crying in 2015. His void never filled. More thanks to the RCB saga. You don’t want to experience that void. Ugh.

PS : I have always been a CSK fan.
