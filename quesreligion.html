Ever Questioned Religion?

This is a thought that dates long long back. But I only got the idea of writing this while talking about this to my friend, in my college dining hall, today.

Reflecting on the earliest memories from my childhood, I cannot recall a version of myself exhibiting faith. Can you recall from your childhood, what was your stance during events of religious significance?

Well, the earliest memory I have is when I was 11 years old. I remember, I had believed that I was an atheist. Why? Because I did not believe in God? How did I know that? Because I had some questions, and I could not find answers, and the best I tried was my friends, my parents, and grandparents. They couldn’t answer my questions, and that was my limit, at least I thought it was. So, I started believing that I was an atheist. Oh, they were very logical questions. So logical, that even today I do not have answers to them. It’s just that now I am not dumb enough to call myself atheist because of my lack of knowledge.

The questions were simple, how complex could an 11-year-old have thought. But, they seem like questions even today. Religion, irrespective of whether you believe in the beliefs or not, has a lot of hypotheses, and so the questions are going to be hypothetical as well, so please bear with me, and whichever side you belong to, take these as questions from a dumb guy. But do try to answer, as logically as you can, to yourself.

First, why all this suffering?

We keep hearing all these accidents, massacres, catastrophes, crimes and everything. But think of it, if there is someone infinitely powerful, watching all this, why don’t they interfere?

“Satyug is the first of the four yugas, or ages, in the Hindu Yuga Cycle. It is considered the most virtuous age, marked by high standards of morality, righteousness, and spirituality. The name 'Satya' means truth.”

Why couldn't Satyug last forever? Mythology says that we humans did all bad to us, and came all the way towards this sadist world. But why not take the ability of doing this away from us?

I understand the dumbness of this question. It’s just never ever I found an answer that rejected this question with logic. I am waiting to be rejected, just give a fair enough reason.

Draupadi in Mahabharat

I always questioned why didn’t Shri Krishna save Draupadi and Pandavas. I know, I know, he saved her from during the Cheer Haran. What happened next? They went into exile for 15 years. And yes, in the end, good won over evil. But what did they do to deserve all those sufferings?

“According to the United Nations Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO), 10% of the world's population, or 828 million people, go to bed hungry each night.”
“Over 370 million girls and women globally subjected to rape or sexual assault as children” – UNICEF

Why?

I don’t understand. If we are the children of God, then why not help?

Coming to Karma

Okay, coming a bit out of the hypotheses. Let me get Karma. Let me get my punishments for whatever bad I have done. But does that justify rapes? The worst, most haunting experience that a person can go through. How can Karma justify this?

Why not bring some order? Why let the Jeffrey Epstein case happen at all?

Final Thoughts

These are just some thoughts. And I don’t straightaway call myself an atheist now. I realised knowledge of religion is the first step towards getting answers, and this is going to be a long journey, and I have started that journey.

By the way, my mom did not expect this change in me, reading about religion and mythology. Let’s see what I discover.

We should question things. Many of us do. We should then actually look for answers. Rarely, we do.
